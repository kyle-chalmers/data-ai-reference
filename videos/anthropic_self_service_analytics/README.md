# Anthropic Self-Service Analytics

Diagram from the "Anthropic Self-Service Analytics" video on the [Kyle Chalmers Data & AI channel](https://youtube.com/@kylechalmersdataai). The video is a plain-English, diagram-led walkthrough of Anthropic's own internal playbook for AI-driven self-service analytics (21% to 95%+ accuracy), including its actual templates and its honest limits.

## Diagram

One pannable canvas covering the full argument end to end: why analytics needs different structure than code, Anthropic's four-layer architecture, how a question actually gets routed to an answer, the real templates filled in, and the honest limits (where credible critics push back, and what a maintenance-drift graph looks like in practice).
